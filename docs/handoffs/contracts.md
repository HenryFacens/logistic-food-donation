## Assumptions

- The product references describe behaviors and screens but do not enumerate
  endpoints. The OpenAPI surface was derived from every documented route and
  required flow.
- A donation remains `offered` while timed-out offers are tried sequentially.
  Offer state changes to `timed_out`; the donation changes only when accepted,
  expired, or exhausted into `no_destination`.
- Only one pending and one accepted offer may exist per donation.
- The paid-freight fallback is a visibly simulated request record, not a
  payment or carrier integration.
- Seed dates are fixed in August and September 2026 so the dataset remains
  deterministic and historical relative to Phase 1.
